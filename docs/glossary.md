# Glossary

**Candidate strategy** — A rule or model-backed allocation method under
evaluation.

**Controlled live** — Live execution with explicit capital limits, monitoring,
and kill conditions.

**Dataset card** — Documentation of a dataset's source, version, semantics, and
limitations.

**Decision point** — Historical or live moment at which strategy-visible inputs
are frozen.

**Label** — Finalized information used for training or evaluation but never
available to the strategy at decision time.

**Permanent control** — A stable benchmark retained across experiments.

**Production miner** — A live miner that has passed research, economic,
operational, and controlled-live gates.

**Walk-forward validation** — Repeated chronological training and evaluation on
future windows.
