# RQ-003 Experiment 1 — Direct Deployment Ordering

## Implementation status

Not started.

## Experiment purpose

Determine whether direct candidate ordering by frozen, protocol-published
deployed lamports contains reproducible decision-time winning-square
information relative to the permanent RQ-003 uninformed baselines.

## Research question

Does direct ordering of candidate squares by deployed lamports available at
the frozen decision observation rank the finalized winning square better than
the deterministic and seeded-random uninformed baselines on chronologically
held-out rounds?

## Scientific motivation

The [Deployment Share equivalence result](../investigations/rq003-deployment-share-order-equivalence.md)
establishes that within-board Share normalization cannot change direct
deployed-lamport ordering. Whether that common ordering contains reproducible
decision-time winning-square information remains empirically unknown and is a
scientifically valid candidate for the first RQ-003 experiment.
