# RFC-002B: Stability and Attribution

## Purpose

Test whether the RFC-002 candidate patterns survive chronological segmentation
and whether square 20 disproportionately explains the corner result.

## Fixed candidates

- congestion quintile q4
- corner × q3/q4
- corner × miner rank 13–21
- excluding neighbor-congestion q5

No new candidate is selected in this RFC.

## Validation design

- first 50%: development
- next 25%: validation
- final 25%: confirmation
- additional ten-part temporal stability view
- square 20 exclusion
- per-corner attribution

## Advancement standard

A candidate should show directionally consistent performance beyond development,
remain meaningful after square 20 exclusion, and avoid dependence on one brief
time segment.
