# ORE Miner V3 Research Journal — 2026

## 2026-07-23 — Transition to Dataset-First Research

### Context

Phase 1 established a validated and optimized replay engine. The first square
feature dataset was generated from 439 source rounds, with 437 accepted rounds
and 10,925 rows.

### Observation

Replay infrastructure was no longer the primary bottleneck. The next meaningful
questions were statistical and economic.

### Decision

Adopt a dataset-first quantitative research workflow.

### Interpretation

The additional process is justified only if it improves the probability of
producing a miner that can be used with real capital. The project will therefore
remain outcome-oriented: research artifacts must progressively reduce
uncertainty about a practical miner.

### Follow-Up

Implement RFC-001, review the first EDA report, and use its findings to decide
the next research question.
